Guest Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 25, 2026

At Kolachi Restaurant Group, we are committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of our Clifton Beach diner guests. This Privacy Policy outlines the information we collect and securely maintain through our digital hosting representative portal.

1. Information We Collect Securely

To provide a premium seaside reservation and interactive support desk experience, we process minimal, necessary client information:

  • Profile Data: When registering, we store your email address and display name securely inside our Supabase database to maintain custom profile setups.
  • Chat Conversations: Dialogue with our AI representative is recorded strictly to maintain active guest threads and allow historical context recall when clicking dining threads.

2. Data Protection & Hosting Security

All client data is encrypted and saved securely inside normalized SQL tables utilizing state-of-the-art secure cloud infrastructures. We enforce strict role policies:

  • Diner conversation history is private and accessible strictly by your authenticated profile session.
  • Guest relations managers hold secure admin privileges strictly to audit telemetry stats, system instrucs, and active inference logging metrics.

3. Cookies & Session Management

We utilize lightweight session data variables to securely maintain active authentication configurations. These sessions are destroyed instantly upon clicking sign out, keeping your digital footprint private.

4. Guest Rights & Database Deletion

Under active client safety regulations, guests hold the complete right to update their display metadata inside `/settings` or request database record flushes from the coastal support desk. Contact our Clifton operations console for assistance.
